#When to do these

Thursday. Not Friday — several of these touch working code, and Juice and Game Feel already warns about breaking a game the night before a showcase.

Two exceptions that belong on Day 3, because they're design decisions and not polish:

  • Two-Player and Gamepad — whether a game is two-player shapes everything else
  • High Scores That Persist — needs Autoload and Global first

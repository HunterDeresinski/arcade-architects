This is the most important idea in Godot. Get this and the rest is details.

#Nodes = LEGO bricks

A node is one small building block that does one job:

  • Sprite2D / AnimatedSprite2D — show a picture
  • Area2D — detect when things overlap (see Area2D)
  • CharacterBody2D — a thing that moves and bumps into walls
  • Label — show text like a score (Score and UI)
  • Timer — wait, then do something (Spawning and Timers)
  • AudioStreamPlayer — play a sound (AudioStreamPlayer)

#Scenes = a tree of nodes

You snap nodes together into a tree (one parent, children underneath) to make a scene. The Day 1 Player scene looks like:

Player            (Area2D)            ← the root, detects hits
├── AnimatedSprite2D                  ← what you see
└── CollisionShape2D                  ← its hit-box shape

For Day 3+ team games, a CharacterBody2D player is more typical:

Player            (CharacterBody2D)   ← the root, it moves + collides
├── AnimatedSprite2D                  ← what you see
└── CollisionShape2D                  ← its hit-box

Children move with their parent. Move the Player and the sprite, hit-box, and hurtbox all come along. That's why the tree matters.

#Scenes inside scenes (instancing)

A scene can be placed inside another scene — that's instancing. You build one Enemy scene, then spawn 50 copies ("instances") of it in the Main scene. Build once, reuse everywhere. This is how spawning works.

Typical project:

Main              ← the game itself
├── Player        (an instance of Player.tscn)
├── UI            (score Label, etc.)
└── (enemies get spawned in here while playing)

#How to do it in the editor

  • Add a node: select the parent, click ➕ in the Scene dock, pick the type.
  • Make a node its own scene: right-click it → Save Branch as Scene. Now you can reuse it.
  • Add a saved scene into another: click the chain/instance icon in the Scene dock, or drag the .tscn from FileSystem into the tree.
💡Rule of thumb

If you'll need more than one of something (enemies, coins, bullets), make it its own scene so you can spawn copies.

The hub for the whole camp. Start at the overview & schedule, then follow the day notes. Each concept note is a standalone reference students can read instead of re-watching the video, with full copy-paste Godot 4 code. The Godot Reference notes are student-facing — the teaching guidance for all of them is collected in Godot Reference - Instructor Notes.

💡How to use this in class

Put the day note on the projector. Students keep the matching concept notes open in Obsidian as their reference. When someone gets stuck on "how do I make it move again?", they open Movement and Input instead of asking you to rewind the video.

  • Day 1 - Meet Godot — build Game #1 (Dodge the Creeps)
  • Day 2 - Make It Yours — remix into Game #2
  • Day 3 - Design Day — pitch, teams, prototype
  • Day 4 - Production Day — build the team game
  • Day 5 - Polish and Showcase — finish + present (4–6 PM)

#Godot Reference

The "how do I actually do it" notes, in four folders. Each has full copy-paste Godot 4 code.

#🧱 0 — Foundations

The concepts that aren't nodes or mechanics. Read the first four before anything else.

Godot Editor Tour · Scenes and Nodes · GDScript Basics · Signals · Tweens · Exporting Your Game

#🧩 1 — Node Info

"What is this node and what do people do with it?" One note per node.

  • Physics & collision — CharacterBody2D · Area2D · RigidBody2D and StaticBody2D · CollisionShape2D · Collision Layers and Masks
  • Visuals & camera — AnimatedSprite2D · AnimationPlayer · Camera2D · TileMapLayer

#🎮 2 — Mechanics

"I want to build a thing — how?" Complete builds. Hand a student this index on Day 3.

  • Moving around — Movement and Input · Platformer Physics · Dash and Double Jump · Moving Platforms · Bouncing Ball
  • Things in the world — Collectables · Obstacles · Hazards · Doors and Keys
  • Enemies & combat — Patrol Enemies · Homing Enemies · Enemy Formations · Making Bullets · Health and Lives
  • Making it a real game — Spawning and Timers · Score and UI · Scene Switching · Autoload and Global · Juice and Game Feel

#🧰 3 — Extras

Everything you don't need — and some of it you'll really want. Sorted into tiers by difficulty and payoff.

  • Tier 2 — Showcase Payoff — Parallax Backgrounds · Particles · High Scores That Persist · Two-Player and Gamepad · Pause Menu
  • Tier 3 — Genre Unlocks — Dialogue and Text Boxes · Grid-Based Movement · Screen Wrap and Thrust · Melee Attacks · Enemy States
  • Tier 4 — Worth Knowing — RayCast2D · Path2D and PathFollow2D · Lights and CanvasModulate · Line2D · Sprite2D · VisibleOnScreenNotifier2D
  • Tier 5 — After Camp — State Machines · Shaders · Save Systems · Pathfinding · Multiplayer Networking
💡Three ways in

If a student knows what they want to build, send them to Mechanics Index — it has a genre table that turns a pitch into a short reading list. If they're asking "what does this node do?", send them to Node Info Index. If they want to go further than the camp needs, send them to Extras Index.

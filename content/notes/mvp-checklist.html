🏠 Curriculum Map · Used on Day 4 - Production Day, Day 5 - Polish and Showcase · 🖨️ Print one per team; tape to the monitor.

MVP = Minimum Viable Product — the smallest version of your game someone can actually play. On Day 4 you build to this list and nothing more. Polish is Day 5.

#The MVP bar (all four = playable)

  • Start — the game begins (a key, a button, or it just runs)
  • Core loop works — the player can do the main action (Tiny Game Design)
  • Win or lose — there's a clear end (you survived / you crashed / you scored enough)
  • Restart — the player can play again without quitting (Scene Switching)

If a stranger can sit down, play, reach a win or loss, and go again without you explaining — you have an MVP. That's the whole Day 4 goal.

#Your team's build list (fill in)

Write the 3–5 specific pieces your game needs. Example for a coin-catcher:

  • Player moves left/right (Movement and Input)
  • Coins fall from the top (Spawning and Timers)
  • Catching a coin adds score (Area2D + Score and UI)
  • Miss 3 coins = game over (Scene Switching)
  • Press a key to restart

#NOT in the MVP (this is Day 5 polish)

Multiple levels · fancy art · particles · screen shake · menus beyond a title · story · extra enemy types. Cross these out of your brain until the MVP is done. (Day 5 - Polish and Showcase)

#Day 4 scope cut

At end of day, cross off anything you won't finish by Friday 3 PM. Get an instructor signature. After today: no new features, polish only.

⚠️The trap

"It would be so cool if it also had ___." Maybe! Write it on a "someday" list and keep building the MVP. Cool unfinished games don't get shown Friday; finished simple ones do (The Tiny Game Rules).

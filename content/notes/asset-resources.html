🏠 Curriculum Map · Used all week, especially Day 2 - Make It Yours and Day 4 - Production Day

Free art, sound, and music you're allowed to use. Check the license — these are all free for projects like ours, but always read the "license" note on a page before using it.

#🎨 Art (sprites, backgrounds, icons)

  • Kenney.nl — huge packs of clean game art, totally free, no attribution required. Best first stop.
  • OpenGameArt.org — big community library (check each item's license).
  • itch.io game assets — tons of free sprite packs.
  • Piskel — free in-browser tool to draw your own pixel art sprites and animations.

#🔊 Sound effects

  • jsfxr — click a preset (coin, jump, hit, powerup), get a retro sound instantly, download .wav. (AudioStreamPlayer)
  • ChipTone — more control, same idea, in-browser.
  • Freesound.org — recorded sounds (account needed; check licenses).

#🎵 Music

  • Kenney music packs — free loops, no attribution.
  • Incompetech — Kevin MacLeod's royalty-free music (attribution required — credit him).
  • BeepBox — make your own chiptune music loop in the browser, free.

#How to use a downloaded asset

  1. Download the file (.png for art, .wav/.ogg for sound, .ogg for music).
  2. Drag it into your project's FileSystem dock in Godot.
  3. Art → set it as a Sprite's texture. Sound → set it as an AudioStreamPlayer's stream (AudioStreamPlayer).
  4. On a team, route asset files through your main computer.
💡Make a few of your own

Even one self-drawn sprite (Piskel) or one self-made sound (jsfxr) makes a game feel personal. Mix found + made.

⚠️Licenses matter

"Free" can still mean "credit the author." If a page says attribution is required (like Incompetech), put the author's name on your title or credits screen. When in doubt, use Kenney — no attribution needed.

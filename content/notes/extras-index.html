Everything you don't need — and some of it you'll really want. The core reference covers what a camp game requires. This is the browsable pile of everything else, sorted by how hard it is and how much it pays off.

🗒️Three notes graduated out of here

Tweens, AnimationPlayer, and Autoload and Global started in Extras and were promoted into Foundations, Node Info, and Mechanics — the core reference already depended on all three. Everything still in this folder is genuine extension material.

💡How to use this

Don't read it front to back. Find the tier that matches how much time you have left, and skim. Every note tells you its difficulty, its payoff, and roughly how long it takes at the top.

#Physics and collision

NodeWhat it's for
CharacterBody2DAnything you move with code — the player, walking enemies
Area2DThe detector. Pickups, hurtboxes, triggers, hazards. Never blocks
RigidBody2D and StaticBody2DWalls that never move, and objects the physics engine throws around
CollisionShape2DThe shape that gives all of the above a body. Forget this and nothing works
Collision Layers and MasksWhich things are allowed to touch which things. The canonical layer scheme for the camp

NodeWhat it's for

Camera2DMaking the screen follow the player

A tiny game is a short game with tight constraints, made fast, built around a single idea. That's the whole camp. These three rules keep you out of trouble:

#1. One core idea

If you can't say what your game is in one sentence, it's too big. Cut until you can. (See One-Sentence Pitch.)

#2. Playable in under 3 minutes

A whole play — start to win-or-lose — should fit in about 3 minutes. Short games get finished and get played.

#3. Done is better than perfect

Cut features, not deadlines. A small game that works beats a big game that's broken. Every time.

💬The mantra

One idea. Three minutes. Done beats perfect.

These come from the spirit of CU Boulder's Tiny Game Studio — fluency through constraints. Constraints aren't a limitation here; they're the point.

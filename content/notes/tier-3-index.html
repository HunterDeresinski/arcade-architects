#Two worth pointing at directly

Grid-Based Movement if a team is anxious about finishing. Puzzle games are the most reliably shippable genre here — there's no feel to tune, and ten small levels is a complete game.

Dialogue and Text Boxes if a team has students who came in through Twine. It's the bridge from writing, which they already know how to do, to Godot.
